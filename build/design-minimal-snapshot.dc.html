EXPERT TEAM FOCUSED ON ONE GOAL
No Evidence of Disease
At NED Labs, we design tailored clinical trials around one patient: you, combining a dedicated research team, continuous measurement and every treatment option that exists.
Every minute counts, so we practice oncology at a different pace: tracking your biology every day to detect early treatment response, and iterating until we find the combination that works for you.
HOW IT WORKS
1
Strategy. An expert research team deep-dives into your medical information and builds a coherent plan, with clear reasoning behind every step.
2
Measurement. Dense daily datasets feed a live dashboard of your personal status and biology, so we see treatment response weeks earlier.
3
Treatment. Existing and novel therapies, personalized and iterated according to state-of-the-art research, until we find what works for you.
SCIENTIFIC MODEL: THE TWO-LOOPS
The n=1 loop combines knowledge, team, and facilities into a loop fast enough to find what works for you personally.
The Humanity loop learns from every patient what worked in their case, becoming smarter and better for the next one. Every n=1 loop serves the larger one.
